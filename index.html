Who We Are

Trans Infra & Logistics is a project-cargo partner for manufacturers, EPCs, and infrastructure leaders across India. We combine ODC & heavy-haul transportation, logistics planning and compliance, and all-capacity crane rentals into one accountable team. Our specialists handle route feasibility, permits, pilot vehicles, rigging plans, and on-site supervision, delivering safe lifts and on-time moves from plant to project site. With transparent coordination and milestone tracking, we keep complex moves simple—and predictable.

What We Do

We move India’s toughest loads—end to end.

  • ODC & heavy‑haul transport: route surveys, permits, escorts, last‑mile.
  • Project logistics: planning, documentation, multimodal options, vendor coordination.
  • Crane rentals: crawler, hydraulic, pick‑n‑carry—short/long term with certified operators.
  • On‑site execution: rigging plans, erection, lift supervision, shutdowns & plant relocations.

Why We Do It Better

One accountable team, zero handoffs.

  • Permit playbooks: pre‑surveyed corridors for faster approvals.
  • Safety first: engineered lift plans, SOPs, audited crews.
  • Control tower: real‑time milestones, clear ETAs, PODs.
  • Predictable costs: transparent pricing, SLAs, no surprises.

How We Succeed

Process, discipline, and visibility—every time.

  • Plan: Feasibility, method statement, crane charts, and risk register.
  • Prepare: Permits, escorts, route conditioning, and site readiness.
  • Protect: Safety briefings, SOP checklists, and compliance monitoring.
  • Perform: Live coordination, exception handling, on‑time delivery with PODs.
