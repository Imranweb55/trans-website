Who We Are

Trans Infra & Logistics

Trans Infra & Logistics Pvt Ltd is a Chennai-headquartered project cargo and heavy-haul company, moving India's toughest loads—wind turbine blades and nacelles, transformers, generators, boilers, and other over-dimensional cargo—on engineered routes, backed by an owned fleet of trailers, hydraulic axles and cranes.

From route survey and permits to execution and on-site positioning, our teams plan every movement in detail so that your project stays on schedule, whatever the terrain.
